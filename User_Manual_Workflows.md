 User Manual - System Workflows

This document outlines the workflows for the different user roles interacting with the Web Learner and Web Admin platforms: Learner, Admin, Lecture, and Staff.

---

 A. LEARNER WORKFLOWS (WEB LEARNER)

The Learner role interacts primarily with the Web Learner platform to study, track progress, interact with AI, and manage their account.

 3.2 Authentication Workflow 

 3.2.1 Register a new account (Registration)
       Step 1: Access the "Registration" page.
       Step 2: Enter your Full name.
           Users enter their Full name in the Full name field.
           This full name will be used to name the account.
       Step 3: Enter your email address.
           Users enter a valid email address in the Email field.
           This email will be used to verify the account using an OTP code.
       Step 4: Create Password.
           The user enters a password in the Create Password (Tạo mật khẩu) field.
           The password should meet the system’s security requirements.
       Step 5: Confirm Password.
           The user re-enters the password in the Confirm Password (Xác nhận mật khẩu mới) field.
           This ensures the password is entered correctly.
       Step 6: Submit Registration.
           Click the Sign Up (Đăng ký) button.
           The system processes the registration request.
       Step 7: Alternatively, users can register using other methods (Google Account and Facebook Account)
           Users can also click on "Google" or "Facebook".
           The system will redirect to the Google or Facebook verification page.
           After successful verification, the account will be created automatically.

 3.2.2 Log in with your account
       Step 1: Access the Login Page.
           Users open the system's website and select the "Đăng Nhập" button on the homepage. The system will display the login form.
       Step 2: Enter Email Address.
           Users enter their registered email address in the Email field.
       Step 3: Enter Password.
           Users enter their password in the Password field (created during registration).
       Step 4: Click Login.
           After entering the necessary information, users click the Login button to submit their login request.
       Step 5: Log in with Google or Facebook (Optional).
           Instead of using email and password, users can choose to Log in with Google or Facebook. The system will redirect users to the Google or Facebook verification page and log them in after successful verification.
       Step 6: Login Results.
           If the login information is correct, the system will authenticate the user and redirect them to the dashboard or main page. If the information is incorrect, the system will display an error message and ask the user to try again.

 3.3 Learning Workflow (Học Tập)

 3.3.1 Explore and Enroll in Courses (Khám phá khóa học)
       Step 1: On the sidebar, click on "Khám phá khóa học".
       Step 2: Browse the available courses or use the search filter.
       Step 3: Select a course to view detailed information (syllabus, instructor, price).
       Step 4: Click "Enroll" (Đăng ký học) or proceed to checkout if it is a paid course.

 3.3.2 Access Enrolled Courses (Khóa học của tôi)
       Step 1: Click on "Khóa học của tôi" to view the list of purchased/enrolled courses.
       Step 2: Select a course to enter the learning interface.
       Step 3: View Video on Demand (VOD) lessons, read materials, or check resources.

 3.3.3 View Schedule & Manage Classes (Thời khóa biểu)
       Step 1: Select "Thời khóa biểu" to see a calendar view of upcoming live classes.
       Step 2: Click on a scheduled session to view details or join the meeting.
       Step 3: (If needed) Select "Reschedule" to submit a class reschedule request.

 3.3.4 Take JLPT Mock Exams (Thi thử JLPT)
       Step 1: Go to "Thi thử JLPT".
       Step 2: Select an available Mock Exam corresponding to the target level.
       Step 3: Complete the exam within the given time limit.
       Step 4: Submit the exam and review the detailed score and explanations.

 3.3.5 Use Flashcards (Thẻ ghi nhớ)
       Step 1: On the sidebar, click on "Thẻ ghi nhớ" to open the flashcards feature.
       Step 2: Select a vocabulary deck to review.
       Step 3: Interact with the flashcards to view phonetic spelling, audio pronunciation, and translations.
       Step 4: Mark cards as remembered or needing review to optimize your study progress.

 3.3.6 Manage My Folder (Thư mục của tôi)
       Step 1: Navigate to "Thư mục của tôi".
       Step 2: View all your saved resources, notes, or bookmarks.
       Step 3: Create predefined folders or organize your study materials efficiently.

 3.3.7 Read Knowledge Blog (Blog kiến thức)
       Step 1: Select "Blog kiến thức" from the sidebar.
       Step 2: Browse through available articles, Japanese learning tips, and news.
       Step 3: Click on a blog post to read its full content.

 3.4 AI Sensei Workflow (AI Sensei)

 3.4.1 Chat with AI (Chat với AI)
       Step 1: Select "Chat với AI".
       Step 2: Type your questions regarding Japanese vocabulary, casual topics, or learning methods.
       Step 3: Receive instant conversational responses and suggestions from the AI.

 3.4.2 Translation & Grammar (Dịch thuật & Ngữ pháp)
       Step 1: Select "Dịch thuật & Ngữ pháp".
       Step 2: Enter the sentence you need to translate or the grammar structure you need help with.
       Step 3: Review the detailed AI analysis breaking down the sentence structure, vocabulary, and direct translation.

 3.4.3 Roleplay Conversation (Hội thoại nhập vai)
       Step 1: Choose "Hội thoại nhập vai".
       Step 2: Select a real-life scenario to practice (e.g., At the restaurant, Interview).
       Step 3: Practice communication via chat or voice with the AI Sensei.

 3.4.4 Subscribe to AI Services (Gói dịch vụ AI)
       Step 1: Go to "Gói dịch vụ AI" to view available AI subscription tiers.
       Step 2: Select a plan, complete payment, and unlock unlimited AI interactions.

 3.5 Progress & Gamification (Tiến độ)

 3.5.1 View Analytics (Analytics)
       Step 1: Click on "Analytics" in the sidebar.
       Step 2: Review personal statistics such as study streak, total learning hours, and completion rates.

 3.5.2 View Leaderboard (Bảng xếp hạng)
       Step 1: Access "Bảng xếp hạng".
       Step 2: View your current standing and total points compared to other Learners in the system.

 3.5.3 View Achievements (Thành tích)
       Step 1: Go to "Thành tích".
       Step 2: View the list of badges and milestones you have achieved.
       Step 3: Keep track of the requirements for upcoming achievements to earn more points.

 3.5.4 Redeem Rewards (Cửa hàng quà tặng)
       Step 1: Click on "Cửa hàng quà tặng".
       Step 2: Browse the available gifts or vouchers.
       Step 3: Use your points accumulated from your "Thành tích" to redeem the reward of your choice.

 3.5.5 View Certificates (Chứng chỉ)
       Step 1: Select "Chứng chỉ".
       Step 2: Review the certificates you have earned upon completing courses or passing system exams.

 3.6 Account Settings Workflow (Tài khoản)

 3.6.1 Manage Learning Profile (Hồ sơ học tập)
       Step 1: Access "Hồ sơ học tập".
       Step 2: View and edit personal information, profile picture, and display name.
       Step 3: Save the updated information to reflect the changes globally.

 3.6.2 Submit Review & Feedback (Đánh giá & Phản hồi)
       Step 1: Select "Đánh giá & Phản hồi".
       Step 2: Choose a course or system feature you wish to review.
       Step 3: Write your feedback, assign a rating, and submit it to the administration to help improve the system.

 3.6.3 View Payment History (Lịch sử thanh toán)
       Step 1: Go to "Lịch sử thanh toán".
       Step 2: Monitor your past transactions, invoices, and active subscriptions.

 3.6.4 Submit Support Tickets (Hỗ trợ)
       Step 1: Access "Hỗ trợ".
       Step 2: Fill out a new support request detailing your issue (e.g., technical problem, billing).
       Step 3: Submit the ticket and track the staff's response from this page.

 3.6.5 Adjust Settings (Cài đặt)
       Step 1: Go to "Cài đặt".
       Step 2: Configure application preferences (notifications, UI theme).
       Step 3: Change your password if needed for security purposes.

 B. SYSTEM WORKFLOWS (WEB ADMIN)

The Web Admin platform is utilized by Admin, Staff, and Lecture roles. 
Their access to the modules below is determined by their assigned Role Permissions.

 3.7 Admin Workflows (Toàn quyền quản trị)
Admin has unrestricted access to the entire platform including User and System Management.

 3.7.1 Authentication Workflow (Admin)
       Step 1: Access the Admin Login portal.
       Step 2: Enter Administrator email address and password.
       Step 3: Click Login. 
       Step 4: System authenticates and initiates session with full access ("Toàn quyền quản trị").

 3.7.2 Manage Academy (LMS)
       Kho Khóa học (Profiles):
           Step 1: Navigate to "Academy (LMS)" -> "Kho Khóa học".
           Step 2: Create, edit, and manage the general profiles of all courses in the system.
       Đợt khai giảng (Cohorts):
           Step 1: Access "Đợt khai giảng".
           Step 2: Manage intake sessions/cohorts for courses, set enrollment periods, and track assigned students.
       Lớp học Live (Live Classes):
           Step 1: Select "Lớp học Live".
           Step 2: Schedule and manage live sessions, assign Lecturers, and monitor attendance.
       Khóa học VOD (Video On Demand):
           Step 1: Go to "Khóa học VOD".
           Step 2: Upload video materials and sequence lessons for self-paced courses.
       Approval Center:
           Step 1: Open "Approval Center".
           Step 2: Review and approve/reject critical requests such as class rescheduling initiated by Lectures or Learners.

 3.7.3 Manage Question Bank & Assessments (Ngân hàng & Đánh giá)
       Ngân hàng câu hỏi (Question Bank):
           Step 1: Navigate to "Ngân hàng & Đánh giá" -> "Ngân hàng câu hỏi".
           Step 2: Create, categorize, and edit individual quiz questions.
       Danh sách bài thi (Exam List):
           Step 1: Go to "Danh sách bài thi".
           Step 2: Assemble questions into structured tests/exams and configure test settings.
       Study Set Catalogs:
           Step 1: Access "Study Set Catalogs".
           Step 2: Organize and group learning materials (like flashcards) into distinct sets for Learners.

 3.7.4 Manage JLPT Mock Exams
       Quản lý Đề thi (Templates):
           Step 1: Navigate to "JLPT Mock Exams" -> "Quản lý Đề thi".
           Step 2: Create and configure templates mimicking real JLPT structures for each level.
       Ngân hàng Câu hỏi (JLPT Question Bank):
           Step 1: Go to "Ngân hàng Câu hỏi" under the JLPT section.
           Step 2: Manage specific questions targeted solely for mock exams.
       Master Mondai:
           Step 1: Access "Master Mondai".
           Step 2: Define and manage the parent problem sets (Mondai) containing sub-questions.
       JLPT Config:
           Step 1: Open "JLPT Config".
           Step 2: Adjust overarching rules, scoring weights, and passing criteria for the JLPT exams.

 3.7.5 User and Role Management (Nhân sự)
       Quản lý Người dùng:
           Step 1: Navigate to "Quản lý Người dùng".
           Step 2: Review the list of all registered accounts (Learners, Staff, Lectures).
           Step 3: Verify accounts, update statuses, or ban users if necessary.
       Phân quyền (Roles):
           Step 1: Go to "Phân quyền".
           Step 2: Create new administrative accounts and assign appropriate roles (e.g., Staff or Lecture).

 3.7.6 System Audit (Hệ thống)
       Nhật ký hệ thống:
           Step 1: Select "Nhật ký hệ thống".
           Step 2: Review operation logs to track actions made by staff or lectures in real-time.

 3.8 Lecture Workflows (Giảng viên)
Lectures have restricted access and primarily interact with Training modules.

 3.8.1 Manage Academy / LMS (Đào tạo - Academy LMS)
       Lớp học Live:
           Step 1: Log into the Web Admin and access "Academy (LMS)" -> "Lớp học Live".
           Step 2: View assigned live classes, start meetings, and manage session details.
       Khóa học VOD:
           Step 1: Navigate to "Khóa học VOD".
           Step 2: Upload supplementary materials or manage lessons for assigned VOD courses.
       Approval Center:
           Step 1: Open "Approval Center".
           Step 2: Submit "Class Reschedule Requests" for approval, or review requests assigned to you by the Admin.

 3.8.2 Manage Question Bank & Assessments (Ngân hàng & Đánh giá)
       Ngân hàng câu hỏi:
           Step 1: Go to "Ngân hàng & Đánh giá" -> "Ngân hàng câu hỏi".
           Step 2: Contribute new questions or edit existing ones relevant to your subject.
       Danh sách bài thi:
           Step 1: Access "Danh sách bài thi" to review or assist in assembling exams for your assigned cohorts.
       Study Set Catalogs:
           Step 1: Go to "Study Set Catalogs" to curate custom study materials like flashcards for your students.

 3.9 Staff Workflows (Nhân viên vận hành & kinh doanh)
Staff members handle daily operations, customer support, and financial tracking.

 3.9.1 Publish Posts & News (Bài viết & Tin tức)
       Step 1: Navigate to "Bài viết & Tin tức" under Vận hành.
       Step 2: Create educational blogs, promotional news, or system announcements.
       Step 3: Format the content, set publication dates, and publish them to the Web Learner platform.

 3.9.2 Handle Support Tickets (Yêu cầu hỗ trợ)
       Step 1: Go to "Yêu cầu hỗ trợ".
       Step 2: Review incoming tickets from Learners regarding account issues, bugs, or questions.
       Step 3: Reply directly to the user and mark the ticket as resolved once the issue is fixed.

 3.9.3 Manage Orders & Revenue (Đơn hàng & Doanh thu)
       Step 1: Navigate to "Đơn hàng & Doanh thu" under Kinh doanh & Tài chính.
       Step 2: Review daily course purchases, AI subscriptions, and financial logs.
       Step 3: Address payment conflicts, refunds, or manual enrollment verifications.

 3.9.4 Manage Coupons (Mã giảm giá)
       Step 1: Select "Mã giảm giá".
       Step 2: Generate new discount codes for marketing campaigns.
       Step 3: Configure usage limits, percentage values, and expiration dates.

 3.9.5 Manage Rewards (Phần thưởng)
       Step 1: Open "Phần thưởng".
       Step 2: Add or update items available in the Learner's "Cửa hàng quà tặng" (Gift Store).
       Step 3: Set the point pricing for each reward item.

 3.9.6 Manage Achievements (Thành tích)
       Step 1: Go to "Thành tích" under Kinh doanh & Tài chính.
       Step 2: Configure the badges and milestones Learners can earn.
       Step 3: Define the point values awarded upon completing these milestones.
